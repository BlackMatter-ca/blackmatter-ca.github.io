Five directions for the home page

Same copy, same design system, five answers to what the first screen should do. Each page has its own theme toggle and remembers its choice.

  1. 1

    The specification

    Reads like the document a client will receive. Numbered clauses, a title block, the contents as navigation. Light by default.

  2. 2

    The territory

    Thirty-one communities projected from their coordinates, linked out from Yellowknife on load. Advice from inside the territory.

  3. 3

    The stone

    The mark at poster scale, filled with the site's own grit texture. Three words under it. Everything else quiet and narrow.

  4. 4

    The record

    Proof before pitch. The first screen is a table of work, scale and outcome. Tabs hold the rest.

  5. 5

    Two grounds

    A fixed dark rail that is the contact card, beside light scrolling content. The toggle swaps the two.
